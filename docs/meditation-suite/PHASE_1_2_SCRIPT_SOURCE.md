**Status:** Complete creative and timing draft for review. Not a command to generate ElevenLabs audio, change the repository or publish.
**Supersedes:** the 10-minute preparation and 25-/35-minute journey times in Voice Script Library v0.1. Retain prior unused clips as an archive, not as active timing assumptions.

## 2. Preparation: ECB

### `suite.ecb.guided.v2` — spoken, ~35–50 sec

Before we begin, notice what has followed you into this moment. A worry. A conversation. Something unfinished.

Imagine a box in front of you. It can look however you like. Open it, and place those concerns inside, one at a time.

You don't need to solve them now. You aren't losing them. You're simply choosing not to carry them for a little while.

Close the box. Leave it where you can find it when you return.

**Direction:** Silence for the balance of 00:00–02:00. Do not announce every concern or narrate the listener's imagination.

### `suite.ecb.familiar.v2` — ~10 sec

Bring your box to mind. Place what's been occupying you inside. Close it, and leave it for later.

## 3. Breathwork: one named technique intro, then the notes

At 02:00, play an approved, soft, **short** derivative of the existing singing bowl, then a technique introduction that finishes before the first paced note. The bowl should be audible as a shift of attention, not a startling notification. For the default five-five body-scan pattern, use the planning grid in section 4.

The exercise introductions below are **alternative assets**, not an instruction to read several in sequence. Each choice is a complete unit. Intro length may need editing to fit the actual reserved window; final measurements govern.

### `suite.breath.intro.5-5.v2` — Phase 1 default

We'll begin with five-five breathing. Five seconds in, five seconds out. Follow the rising and falling notes. With each exhale, we'll give a part of the body room to soften.

### `suite.breath.intro.4-4-6.v2`

Four seconds in, a gentle hold for four, then six seconds out. Follow the notes without straining. As you exhale, let the body settle a little more.

### `suite.breath.intro.4-7-8.v2`

We'll try four-seven-eight breathing for a few complete rounds. Breathe in for four, hold comfortably for seven, and breathe out for eight. Then we'll return to easy, natural breathing as the body relaxes.

**Important:** The 4-7-8 option is a short bout followed by natural relaxation, not ten forced long holds in a row.

### `suite.breath.intro.natural.v2`

There's no count to follow this time. Breathe at your own pace. With each exhale, let another place in the body become a little less busy.

### `suite.breath.intro.box.v2` — future builder

We'll use box breathing: four in, hold for four, four out, and another gentle hold for four. Follow the rising, steady and falling notes, and keep every phase comfortable.

### `suite.breath.intro.4-4-6-2.v2` — future builder

Four seconds in, hold softly for four, six seconds out, then rest for two. The repeated notes mark each hold. You can return to natural breathing whenever you choose.

### `suite.breath.intro.6-6.v2` — future builder

Six seconds in and six seconds out. Let the notes mark an easy, even rhythm.

### `suite.breath.intro.4-6.v2` — future builder

Breathe in for four and out for six. Allow the longer exhale to be soft and unforced.

### `suite.breath.intro.custom.v2` — future builder

Your chosen pattern is ready. Rising notes mean inhale, the repeated pitch means hold, and falling notes mean exhale. Let comfort be more important than the count.

### `suite.breath.change.v2` — future builder, only if an actual new technique starts

The rhythm is changing. Listen for the next set of notes, and begin when you're ready.

**Existing recordings:** Keep the physiological-sigh introduction and “A little more” recording as a separate established cue system; don't replace or duplicate them without a separate review. Do not introduce Hormesis/retention practice into an ordinary meditation journey by default. The undefined 4-4-4-6 pattern still needs its phase order established before a named clip is made.

## 4. Breathwork: progressive release passages

These are spoken **before** a short group of full breath cycles, not on top of a cycle. The listener then uses each following exhale to release attention from the named region. Exact phase changes remain the responsibility of the existing deterministic note engine.

| ID | Spoken text | Focus for subsequent exhales |
|---|---|---|
| `suite.body.face.v2` | With the next few exhales, let your forehead, eyes and jaw soften. | Face and jaw |
| `suite.body.shoulders.v2` | Let your shoulders settle. Give your arms and hands permission to rest. | Shoulders, arms, hands |
| `suite.body.breath.v2` | Notice the movement of your ribs and abdomen. Let the exhale happen without effort. | Ribs and abdomen |
| `suite.body.back.v2` | Feel the support beneath your back and hips. Let yourself be held by it. | Back and hips |
| `suite.body.legs.v2` | Let that softness travel through your legs, all the way to your feet. | Legs and feet |
| `suite.body.whole.v2` | Leave the whole body as it is. Breathe naturally, and notice what's become quieter. | Closing release; can be a caption instead of speech |

### Proposed default 5/5 schedule: complete, conflict-free and exactly 5:00

Local breathing-block time (global time = 02:00 plus values below):

| Local block time | Event |
|---|---|
| 00:00–00:25 | Short bowl → technique intro → brief gap; no paced notes |
| 00:25–01:15 | Face cue, then **4 × 10-second 5/5 cycles** (10-second cue window + 40 seconds of tones) |
| 01:15–02:05 | Shoulders cue, then 4 full cycles |
| 02:05–02:55 | Ribs/abdomen cue, then 4 full cycles |
| 02:55–03:45 | Back/hips cue, then 4 full cycles |
| 03:45–04:35 | Legs/feet cue, then 4 full cycles |
| 04:35–05:00 | Natural breathing and quiet full-body release; optional closing caption, no clipped breath |

**Total:** 25 s opener + five 50 s groups + 25 s release = 300 s. **Twenty full 5/5 cycles**, not the old 18-cycle unvoiced pattern or the previous illustrative 28-cycle example. The current app does not implement this; Work must update the compiler/scheduler and versioned snapshot, preserving backward compatibility.

The ten-second cue windows are **maximum slots**, not targets to stretch speech. Actual short recordings can finish early and leave silence. The initial 25 seconds must contain a measured short bowl, recorded intro and separation gap; if it does not, revise this grid before production. At 5/5, the tone starts only at the beginning of each 40-second practice segment. The breathing pattern recommences only at a full inhale after a cue gap.

### Alternate technique policy

- **4/4/6:** In each 50-second region group allow up to 8 seconds for cue, then **3 complete 14-second cycles = 42 sec**. Five such groups occupy 250 seconds; the same 25/25-second opener/ending keeps the five-minute section exact. ElevenLabs measured line lengths must fit the 8-second cue windows or the schedule must be revised.
- **4/7/8:** Use **four complete 19-second cycles** in two well-separated groups. Remaining time is **natural breathing and relaxation**, with the region prompts scheduled into quiet slots. No silent assumption that the listener should do ten long holds. The UI should call this “four-seven-eight, then natural relaxation.” The precise 300-second event grid must be compiled and verified before release.
- **Natural breathing:** The same five-minute block and region progression, but **no paced notes or spoken counting**. Cue windows and quiet practice may be longer.
- **Future Build Your Own:** Permit multiple techniques as deliberately selected blocks; one bowl/intro at a *technique change*, never each cycle, with all cue time and practice cycles counted in the declared session duration.

## 5. Resonant tuning — 07:00–09:30 (2:30)

### `suite.tuning.guided.v2` — intro ends before active tuning

Now we'll use your own voice. Take an easy breath in. As you breathe out, let it become a comfortable hum or open vowel. Find a pitch that feels natural. Notice the vibration in your chest, throat or face, then let the sound fade. Repeat at your own pace.

### `suite.tuning.familiar.v2`

On the next few exhales, let a gentle hum form. Notice the vibration and the quiet afterward.

### `suite.tuning.silent.v2` — no-humming variant

For this part, you can remain silent. Let the breath move naturally, and listen to the quiet that follows each exhale.

**Exact proposed mini-timeline:** 07:00–07:06 short soft bowl, 07:06–07:45 voice and gap, **07:45–09:15 uninterrupted listener vocal practice (90 sec)**, 09:15–09:30 quiet. If a decoded Guided recording cannot fit within the 39-second spoken/gap window, shorten/rewrite it or adjust the declared budget *without stealing the 90-second practice*. No synthetic breathing tones during active tuning. The underlying Warm bed may continue at unobtrusive volume.

## 6. REBAL — 09:30–12:00 (2:30)

### `suite.rebal.guided.v2`

Bring your attention to the space around your body.

Imagine a current gathering beneath your feet. Let it rise through you, continue beyond the top of your head, and flow outward in a wide, gentle arc.

Allow it to move down around you and return beneath your feet, forming a continuous field.

You may feel it as a sphere, a flowing current, or simply the space surrounding you.

For now, let it continue on its own. Rest within it.

### `suite.rebal.familiar.v2`

Let the field form. Rising through you, flowing outward and around, returning beneath your feet. Rest inside that movement.

### `suite.rebal.nonvisual.v2`

Notice the space immediately around your body. The air, the room, the distance between you and everything nearby. Allow yourself to rest within that space, without needing a picture.

**Budget:** Aim to finish spoken creation by 10:25 at the latest, leaving at least 95 seconds in the 09:30–12:00 section for silence and practice. For a nonvisual setting, substitute its own clip; never tell the person to visualize a field they have opted out of.

## 7. Affirmation selection — 12:00–14:00 (2:00)

The affirmation is a **selected first-person intention**, not a score or promise. Offer four approachable options for every journey: **Neutral**, **Spiritual**, **Classic Gateway**, and **Personal**. The first two change with the journey; Classic Gateway is the same clearly attributed historical choice across all journeys; Personal is the user's own local text or private silent statement. The two-minute slot is reserved in full for either a shorter original affirmation plus silent contemplation or a longer Gateway version.

### `suite.affirmation.leadin.v2` — common, optional

Now bring your intention forward. Let the words settle in your own way.

### The Foundation — Neutral `suite.affirmation.foundation.neutral.v2`

I can rest without losing awareness. I can notice my body becoming quiet while my attention remains open. For this time, I give myself permission simply to be here.

### The Foundation — Spiritual `suite.affirmation.foundation.spiritual.v2`

I am more than the concerns that occupy my ordinary day. I welcome a deeper knowing of myself, and I enter this quiet with gratitude. May whatever I discover be guided by wisdom, love and understanding.

### Beyond the Familiar — Neutral `suite.affirmation.beyond.neutral.v2`

I enter this experience with curiosity and steadiness. My attention can open beyond its familiar habits. I welcome what I notice, without needing to name it or force it to become something else.

### Beyond the Familiar — Spiritual `suite.affirmation.beyond.spiritual.v2`

I enter this experience with an open heart and a clear intention. I welcome understanding beyond my usual perspective, and I am receptive to guidance that serves compassion, wisdom and growth.

### Build Your Own — Neutral `suite.affirmation.builder.neutral.v2`

I know why I've set aside this time. I can return to that intention without forcing an outcome. I will carry whatever is useful back into my ordinary life.

### Build Your Own — Spiritual `suite.affirmation.builder.spiritual.v2`

## 11. Learn / Refresh — instruction outside the 15-minute ritual

The separate educational pathway can explain the methods with detail and a replay button. These instructions are **not inserted into everyday Guided**.

| ID | Proposed spoken content |
|---|---|
| `suite.learn.intro.v2` | We'll learn a simple way to prepare. Set concerns aside, settle the body with breathing, create resonance with your own voice, form a surrounding field, choose an intention, then become quiet. We'll practice each part separately. |
| `suite.learn.ecb.v2` | Your box can take any form. Give it a lid or a door, make it yours, then place something distracting inside. You can open it again later. |
| `suite.learn.breath.v2` | Rising notes mean inhale. Repeated notes at one pitch mean hold. Falling notes mean exhale. Between groups of breaths, a short invitation helps you release another region of the body. The counting remains in the music. |
| `suite.learn.tuning.v2` | Resonant tuning uses your own comfortable hum, not an audio track you imitate. Choose a pitch that feels easy. Notice the vibration, and listen when it fades. The guide will stop talking while you do it. |
| `suite.learn.rebal.v2` | Imagine a current rising from beneath you, passing upward and arching around your body until it returns. Give the image time to take shape. If imagery doesn't come easily, you can simply notice the surrounding space. |
| `suite.learn.affirmation.v2` | Your affirmation is a choice about how to meet the experience. Each journey offers neutral, spiritual, classic Gateway and personal options. You can listen, repeat it inwardly or use your own words. |
| `suite.learn.return.v2` | When the main journey ends, we'll spend three minutes returning: noticing the room, feeling your body, and moving again at a comfortable pace. You can also end early whenever you wish. |
| `suite.refresh.v2` | Set aside your concerns, settle the body through breathing, hum if you choose, establish your surrounding field, and bring your intention to mind. Then let the guidance grow quiet. |

For a beginner, prioritize the ECB, tuning and REBAL explanations. The *Refresh* version may be just a short reminder. Only replay the full Learn content when the listener actively asks for it.
