## Delivery direction

One narrator throughout: warm, calm, inviting, conversational, natural midrange, clear but unforced. Leave ordinary breaths and gentle pauses. Avoid an exaggerated baritone, mystical announcement, whisper performance or continuous motivational talk. Read only the quoted words; all timings and performance directions are unspoken. No voice counting. The musical instrument owns each second.

Use short reusable complete passages, not full-session narration files. Shared preparation and all three return clips serve both journeys and the 13-minute rehearsal. Keep silence and sound generation in the app. Replaying bundled local recordings offline makes no new generation call. Archive every approved take with script/voice/model/settings/ID/hash, exact decoded duration and measured credit usage.

## Timing and silence

ECB stays first for 90 seconds. The breath section stays 01:30–04:30: reserve 20 seconds for the introduction in Guided, Familiar, narration-muted and missing-recording modes. First paced note is at 01:50. Use sixteen 5/5 cycles; eleven 4/4/6 cycles plus six natural seconds; four 4/7/8 cycles plus 84 natural seconds; or natural breathing throughout. No cycle is clipped. All introductions have an 18-second decoded maximum, leaving two seconds before the first note. Actual recordings must be measured; if one fails its slot, revise the script or make an explicit versioned schedule change. The player skips an oversized/missing clip and retains captions.

The remaining preparation is relaxation 04:30–06:30, tuning 06:30–07:30, surrounding space 07:30–08:30, affirmation 08:30–09:00, entry 09:00–10:00. Speak once near each section's start and leave its remaining time quiet. Threshold starts 10:00. Beyond's deepening bowls remain at 18:00 and 27:00, with no voice before 18:16 / 27:16. No added bowls. Return remains three minutes, at 22:00–25:00 or 32:00–35:00, with three separate short cues 60 seconds apart. Every decoded clip also needs at least 0.25 seconds before its slot end; no automatic stretching, truncation or spill into notes.

## Learn and Refresh

The seven lessons below are an interactive teaching route with manual advance, outside the 25/35-minute session. ### Orientation lesson 1

ID: `suite.learn-ecb.2` · suggested maximum: 40s · 292 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> The Energy Conversion Box is a way to acknowledge concerns and set them aside temporarily. You can imagine a container or simply think of a place to return to later. You do not need to suppress a feeling or solve a problem. Try this with one small distraction, and see what feels comfortable.

### Orientation lesson 2

ID: `suite.learn-breath.2` · suggested maximum: 45s · 330 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> One soft note marks each second. Rising notes guide the inhale, and falling notes guide the exhale. Repeated notes at one pitch mark an optional hold. Choose a comfortable pattern before starting. There is no need to take larger breaths, keep up with a difficult hold, or complete a pattern. Natural breathing is always available.

### Orientation lesson 3

ID: `suite.learn-relaxation.2` · suggested maximum: 30s · 248 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> Body relaxation means bringing gentle attention to areas that may be holding effort. You can notice your face, shoulders, hands, and the support beneath you. A feeling of heaviness, stillness, or deep relaxation is not required. Noticing is enough.

### Orientation lesson 4

ID: `suite.learn-tuning.2` · suggested maximum: 30s · 257 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> Resonant tuning here means an optional soft hum on a comfortable out-breath. Use your own pitch and an easy volume. Notice the vibration and the quiet that follows. If you prefer silence, choose the quiet option. Both choices keep the same preparation time.

### Orientation lesson 5

ID: `suite.learn-space.2` · suggested maximum: 30s · 294 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> Surrounding-space awareness can be a gentle imagined sphere or boundary, or simply an awareness of the room. This is a way to direct attention. No vivid image or special sensation is required. Choose the neutral option if you prefer to stay with the support beneath you and the room around you.

### Orientation lesson 6

ID: `suite.learn-affirmation.2` · suggested maximum: 30s · 281 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> An affirmation sets a simple direction for the practice. You can use the open and grounded words, choose the optional spiritual version, or quietly use your own. Nothing private is recorded. Choose words that feel honest rather than something you need to force yourself to believe.

### Orientation lesson 7

ID: `suite.learn-entry.2` · suggested maximum: 30s · 314 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> After preparation, the journey leaves more quiet space. The sound changes gently, and every journey includes three minutes to return. Stop is always available. The rehearsal uses the actual ten-minute preparation and three-minute return, so you can become familiar with the sequence before taking a longer journey.

### Optional standalone refresher

ID: `suite.refresh.2` · suggested maximum: 45s · 342 characters · not inserted into a running journey.

> Begin by setting concerns aside. Follow your chosen breathing rhythm, then let the body rest. Hum softly if you like, notice your surrounding space, and choose a simple intention. Let the instructions fall away as you enter the journey. Natural breathing and Stop remain available, and the final three minutes bring you back at your own pace.
