# Smooth breathing and meditation entry

Implements the user's Steady Ground listening feedback: reduce direction-change crackle, soften the move into meditation, and use the existing recorded singing bowl.

Audible Soft Felt notes receive a 25 ms release when their phase retires, instead of being disconnected at a nonzero sample. The phase entry also refreshes the audio/engine clock mapping to avoid accumulated offset. Retiring oscillators are separately tracked until their end callback; Stop, mute and interruption still clear them immediately. Pitch steps, normal volume, instrument attack/decay and practice cycle counts are unchanged.

The versioned flow routines use one continuous acoustic timeline across natural settling, the transition and the following meditation. For Steady Ground, the environment fades in over the last 15 seconds of settling and the 3-second transition. Enabled practice background levels fade down over the same lead-in. Without a settling block, the environment begins during the 3-second transition and continues a gradual 18-second entry. A smoothstep entry avoids an abrupt slope at either end. The official meditation duration and the whole session deadline do not change, including after early retention release. Silent blocks do not create a bridge. The short transition countdown is replaced with “Settle into the sound.”

New and existing users receive the recorded `Singing Bowl Signal.wav` default at meditation opening and completion. A one-time preference marker permits later explicit selection of the previous chime. Disabled signals and zero signal level stay disabled; lower signal levels remain. New starts of modern saved routines upgrade inherited generic chimes in the playback copy; saved routine records and frozen history are not rewritten. The bowl asset was already available offline. No narration or audio assets were regenerated; no ElevenLabs credits spent.

Validation: 726 tests across 25 files; production build and immutable asset/PWA checks pass. `scripts/verify-smooth-audio.cjs` checks native Chrome at 44.1 and 48 kHz, production note retirement and immediate Stop, one worklet across settling/transition/meditation, one recorded opening and closing bowl, silent future origin, finite smooth worklet output, and one shared AudioContext. At a deliberately nonzero note cut, maximum adjacent-sample jump fell from 0.03195 to 0.00370 at 44.1 kHz and from 0.07647 to 0.00351 at 48 kHz. These are targeted discontinuity measurements, not a promise that every device artifact is eliminated.

The same check performs a real service-worker upgrade from the previous public build, preserves database version, saved routines, history, journal and draft, verifies the intended one-time signal preference change, and starts Steady Ground offline with its cached bowl. Physical phone/headphone listening and screen-lock testing remain user checks. Laboratory DSP, existing journey recordings and original asset hashes remain unchanged.
